The problem

Your plan lives in a binder. Your revenue leaks out of it.

A maintenance plan is the steadiest money a small shop has. Keeping it running by hand is where it falls apart.

  • Renewals slip.Nobody calls when the year is up, and the customer drifts to whoever knocks next.
  • Visits get forgotten.The spring tune-up you promised never gets booked, and the member stops feeling like a member.
  • Billing is a chore.Chasing checks and re-typing card numbers eats an afternoon every month.
  • The big suites cost too much.You don't need a $300/month system to run one plan well.

What it does

One job, done properly: your maintenance plan.

Tuneline doesn't replace your dispatch, invoicing or accounting. It runs the plan, and works alongside whatever you already use.

Signups

Customers join online

A simple signup page with your plan, your price and your logo. Share the link on your site, your invoices, or by text after a service call.

Billing

Card on file, billed for you

Monthly or yearly. Failed cards get retried and the customer gets a friendly nudge, so you don't have to make that call.

Visits

Tune-ups that schedule themselves

Spring and fall visits go on the list automatically. Customers get a text to pick a time; your techs see who is due this week.

Renewals

Renewals on autopilot

Plans renew on their date with the card on file. Customers who need a reminder get one 30 days ahead.

Report

One page a month

Active members, plan revenue, visits done and due, and anyone at risk of lapsing. Readable in a minute.

Support

A direct line to the builder

Founding shops talk to the person writing the software. If something is missing, it gets built.

Questions

Straight answers

Is Tuneline ready today?

Not yet. The first version is being built with a handful of founding shops, and it goes live for them when their pilot starts. If it doesn't fit your shop, you don't pay.

I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Is this for me?

Probably not, if their plan features work for you. Tuneline is for shops that run plans on paper, spreadsheets or QuickBooks, or are stuck on a tier where plan billing is locked.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They get a signup link, text and email reminders, and a page to update their card. That's it.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export your full customer list, visits and billing history as a spreadsheet at any time. It's your list.

Does it work for plumbing plans too?

Yes. Water heater flushes, drain checks and whole-home inspections run the same way as furnace and AC tune-ups.
